# course-setup

Choose a design from the example images, and `course-setup` develops learner pages for your brand and courses based on that direction.

<a id="choose-a-design"></a>

## Choose a design from images

Browse the desktop and mobile screens in the [real example gallery](/template/docs/course/overview). Select an image to inspect the home, course list and course detail screens. Save the screens you like, or your own reference images, in a `references/` folder in your project, and tell your agent what you like about their layout, spacing, typography or colors.

> Start with course-setup. I saved the Money Routine screens in references/. I like its clear course cards and calm green tones. Build the home, course list and course detail pages for my brand and courses.

<a id="complete"></a>

## Develop the chosen design

`course-setup` uses the references to design the existing learner pages: home, course list, course detail, checkout, the lesson player and my courses. It applies your brand while retaining the existing enrollment, payment and progress features. The references guide composition and style; their logos, photos and course content are not copied.

Review and refine the developed screens in Storybook and desktop/mobile previews.
